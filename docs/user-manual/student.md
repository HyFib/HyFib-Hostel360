# Student guide

The hostel portal on your phone: your room, fees and online payment, the mess menu and
bills, mess reduction, home-visit leave, grievances, feedback and certificates — in English
or தமிழ். No password and nothing to install. Tamil quick guide:
[மாணவர் / பெற்றோர் விரைவு வழிகாட்டி](student-parent-quick-guide-ta.md).

## 1. Open the portal

Go to **`https://<portal address>/hostel`** (for ACGCET, e.g. `https://hostel.accet.ac.in/hostel`),
or use the **Student login** button on the hostel website.

To keep it like an app: in Chrome tap ⋮ > *Add to Home screen* (or *Install app*); on an
iPhone, Safari > Share > *Add to Home Screen*.

## 2. Sign in

You can sign in once the hostel office has **validated** your registration. Use the mobile
number you gave the hostel office.

1. **Sign in** screen: enter your 10-digit mobile number, choose **SMS** or **WhatsApp**, tap
   **Send code**.
2. Enter the 6-digit code you receive and tap **Verify and sign in**. The code is valid for 5
   minutes. No code? Wait for **Resend code** (after 60 seconds).
3. If your parent uses the same number, you are asked **Who is signing in?** — tap **I am the
   student**.

Never share your code with anyone; hostel and HyFib staff will never ask for it.

**First sign-in**

1. **Privacy notice**: read how the hostel uses your information and tap **I have read and
   accept**. You are asked again whenever the notice changes.
2. **Confirm your details**: check your profile; correct your e-mail, address and emergency
   contact if needed; tap **These details are correct**.

**"You can't use the portal right now"** means the hostel office has not validated your
registration yet, your record has been closed, or the portal is switched off. The screen shows
the hostel office's phone and e-mail; contact them, then sign in again.

**Locked** after too many wrong codes: wait an hour, or ask the hostel office to unlock your
number.

## 3. Home

Today's summary: **Fees due** (with **Pay now**), **Your room**, **Today's menu**, **Requests in
progress** (leave, mess reductions, grievances, certificates), **Quick actions** and **Latest
updates**. The bottom bar has Home, Fees, Mess, Leave and **More** (everything else).

## 4. Fees and payment

**Fees** shows what is outstanding, what is overdue and the next due date, with every invoice
and its items. **Download fee structure (PDF)** gives the fee structure that applies to you.

To pay online:

1. Tick the invoices to pay (or **Select all**), then **Pay** the total.
2. **Pay securely**: the payment window of the gateway opens (Razorpay inside the page, or
   PayU's page). Pay by UPI, card or net banking.
3. Do not close the page while it says *Confirming your payment…*. You see **Payment
   successful** and the receipt number, and can **Download receipt**.

If the payment fails, no money is taken and you can **Try again**. If money was debited but
the screen says *being confirmed*, wait: the receipt appears within minutes (Hostel360 checks
with the gateway every 15 minutes). Keep the reference shown, and contact HyFib support if it
does not appear by the next day.

**Receipts** lists every payment — at the office or online — with a PDF for each.

## 5. Mess

- **Mess menu**: this week's menu, day by day (breakfast, lunch, snacks, dinner, specials);
  **Previous** / **Next** for other weeks.
- **Mess bills**: each month's bill — days enrolled, reduction days, chargeable days, daily
  rate, mess charge, extras and total — and whether it is paid. **Pay bill** takes you to
  payment.
- **Mess reduction**: when you will be away for at least the minimum number of days (shown on
  the form), apply in advance: **Apply** > from date, to date, reason (Home Visit, Medical,
  Internship, Sports or Cultural Event, College Tour, Other), details > **Submit application**.
  Track its status; **Cancel application** while it is pending. Approved days are deducted
  from that month's mess bill.

## 6. Leave (home visits)

1. **Leave** > **Apply**: leaving date and time, returning date and time (India time),
   destination, reason, a contact number during the leave, and tick **Also apply for mess
   reduction** if you will miss enough mess days.
2. **Submit leave request**. Your parent is asked to approve it in their portal (if the hostel
   requires parent approval), then the warden. **Approval progress** shows each step; you are
   notified of the decision.
3. On the day: show yourself at the gate; security checks you out against the approved
   leave (from 2 hours before it starts). Check in with security when you are back.
4. Plans changed? **Cancel request** while it is pending or approved (a linked mess reduction
   is withdrawn too).

Come back on time: after the return time plus the grace period you are marked **Overdue** and
the warden and your parents are told.

## 7. Grievances

1. **Grievances** > **Raise a grievance**: category (room and furniture, electrical, plumbing,
   cleanliness, mess and food, water, internet, security, health, ragging or harassment,
   staff behaviour, other), a short subject, the description (what, where, since when),
   priority, and up to 5 **photos or documents** (PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP, up to 5 MB each).
2. Tick **Keep this confidential** if you do not want others to know who raised it; **ragging
   or harassment** complaints are always confidential and go to the warden straight away.
   For an emergency call the hostel office, the emergency number or the national
   anti-ragging helpline shown on the form.
3. **Submit grievance**. Follow it under **Updates**; **Add a comment** if staff need to know
   more.
4. When it is resolved, **rate** how it was handled (1–5 stars), or **Reopen grievance**
   within 7 days if the problem is back.

## 8. Feedback

**Feedback** (under More): once a month for each of Mess, Hostel, Facilities and General —
an overall rating (required), ratings for food quality, cleanliness, staff behaviour and
facilities, and comments. Tick **Submit anonymously** to hide your name from hostel staff.

## 9. Certificates

**Certificates** > **Request certificate**: purpose (Bank Loan, Scholarship, Passport, Visa,
Internship, Address Proof, Other) and details (e.g. the bank's name) > **Send request**. When
the warden approves it, **Download PDF**. The certificate carries a QR code that a bank or
embassy can scan to verify it, and shows its validity date.

## 10. Notifications, profile, language

- **Notifications**: approvals, payments, menus, bills, grievance updates; tap one to open it.
  You may also get SMS or WhatsApp messages for important ones.
- **Profile**: your details, room and guardians; contact the hostel office to change anything
  you cannot edit.
- **Language**: the language switch (English / தமிழ்) on the sign-in page, the More page or
  your Profile changes the screens and the messages you receive.
- **Help and support**: the hostel office (rooms, fees, records, rules) and HyFib portal
  support (sign-in problems, payment errors), with phone and e-mail.
- **Sign out** (on your Profile) when you use a shared phone.

## Before you start (staff)

- **Address**: `https://<portal address>/login` (for ACGCET, the address in Hostel Settings,
  e.g. `https://hostel.accet.ac.in/login`). Use Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a computer; the
  Gate Pass page also works on a tablet.
- **Sign-in**: your e-mail and password, then the 6-digit code from your authenticator app
  (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or FreeOTP). At the first sign-in Hostel360
  e-mails you a QR code to scan; the link is valid for 4 minutes, so keep the sign-in page
  open. A new phone: ask the HyFib helpdesk to reset your two-factor key.
- **Five wrong passwords** lock the account for 10 minutes. Passwords must be strong and are
  changed every 180 days. Sessions end after 8 hours.
- **What you see** depends on your role and, for deputy wardens, office, mess and security
  staff, on the hostels (and blocks) you are assigned to in your Hostel Staff record. If
  something you need is missing, ask the Hostel Administrator.
- **Language**: your user settings (top-right avatar > *My Settings* > Language) offer English
  and தமிழ்.
- **Help**: *Hostel Admin > Get Support* raises a ticket with the HyFib helpdesk; or call
  **+91 96060 60596** / e-mail **support@hyfib.com**, 24×7.

## Common desk actions

| To | Do |
|---|---|
| Find a record | Type in the search bar at the top (register number, name, receipt number) or open the list from the workspace and use the filters |
| Export a list or report | Report view or the report page > *Export* (Excel or CSV); print or *PDF* from the menu |
| See who changed what | Open the record and scroll to the timeline at the bottom (every change is recorded) |
| Get notified | The bell icon (top right) shows Hostel360 notifications addressed to you |

# Security staff manual

For **gate security staff** (role *Hostel Security*). You record when students leave on an
approved home-visit leave and when they come back. You see only the hostels (and blocks) you
are posted to, and only what the gate needs: name, register number, room, photo and the
leave. You cannot open student records, fees or reports.

## 1. Sign in at the gate tablet

1. Open the portal address `/login` (for ACGCET, e.g. `https://hostel.accet.ac.in/login`).
2. Your e-mail and password, then the 6-digit code from the authenticator app on your phone.
3. Open **Gate Pass** (the page opens at `/desk/gate-pass`; bookmark it on the tablet).

Do not share your login. Sign out (avatar, top right > *Log out*) at the end of your shift
if the tablet is shared.

## 2. The Gate Pass screen

| Section | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Leaving today** | Students whose approved leave starts today, with the time they are due to leave |
| **Away** | Students checked out and not yet back; **Overdue** students first |
| Search box | Find a student by register number, roll number, name or room number |

**Refresh** reloads the lists. The hostel filter shows your hostels.

## 3. A student is leaving

1. Find the student in *Leaving today* or with the search box.
2. **Check that it is the student** (photo, name, register number, room).
3. Press **Check Out**, then **Confirm Check Out**. The time recorded is the server's current
   time. The student's parents are notified.

You can check a student out from **2 hours before** the leave starts until the leave's end
time. There is no **Check Out** button if the student has no approved leave for now: **do
not let them out**; ask them to contact the warden (the leave may still be waiting for the
parent or the warden, or it has ended and a new leave is needed).

## 4. A student is back

1. Find the student in **Away** or with the search box.
2. Check that it is the student.
3. Press **Mark Returned**, then **Confirm Return**. The time is recorded and the parents are
   notified.

If a student comes back and does not appear as away (they left without being checked out),
search for them: you can still record the return of an approved leave.

## 5. Overdue students

A student not back by the return time plus the grace period (2 hours by default) shows as
**Overdue** at the top of *Away*. The warden and parents are notified automatically. When the
student arrives, record the return as usual.

## 6. If something is wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| The page shows nothing or an error | Press **Refresh**; check the tablet's internet; call the hostel office |
| A student is not in the list | Search by register number; if still not found, their leave is not approved or they are not in your hostel — call the warden |
| You pressed the wrong student | Tell the warden at once; the warden corrects the leave |
| Cannot sign in | Call the HyFib helpdesk **+91 96060 60596** (24×7) |

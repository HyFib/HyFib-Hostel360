# Warden manual

For the **Hostel Warden** (chief warden, all hostels) and **Deputy Wardens** (their assigned
hostels, and blocks if assigned). Read the [staff introduction](README.md#before-you-start-staff)
first.

What differs between the two roles:

| | Warden | Deputy Warden |
|---|---|---|
| Hostels | All | Only those in your Hostel Staff assignments (and only your blocks, if assigned) |
| Leave and bonafide decisions | All students | Students you cover |
| Mess reductions | Always | When Mess Settings names Deputy Warden as the approver |
| Mess indents | Approves (and above the warden threshold) | Read only |
| Confidential grievances (ragging, harassment) | Sees and handles | Only if assigned to you |
| Escalated grievances | Receives every escalation | Receives first-level escalations for your hostels |

## 1. Your daily screen: Approvals

*Student Services > Approvals* (also a shortcut on *Hostel Admin*). One inbox with
everything waiting for you, in sections:

- **Leave** — requests waiting for the warden's decision, with dates, destination, reason,
  contact and the parent's decision ("Parent approved", "Parent approval not required",
  "Applied by parent") and remarks.
- **Mess Reduction** — applications waiting for approval (if you are an approver).
- **Certificates** — bonafide requests with purpose and programme.
- **Mess Indents** — indents pending approval (Warden).
- **Grievances** — escalated, urgent or confidential grievances that need attention, with
  "Action due by".

For each item: **Approve**, or **Reject** with a reason (required), or for a grievance
**Take Ownership** / **Resolve** with what was done. Items you cannot act on are not shown.
"You're all caught up" means nothing is waiting. **Refresh** reloads the inbox.

The workspace number cards show *Pending Leave Approvals*, *Students on Leave Now*, *Open
Grievances* and *Escalated Grievances*.

## 2. Home-visit leave

How a leave moves:

1. The student (or a parent on their behalf) applies in the portal: from/to date and time,
   destination, reason, contact number, and optionally a mess reduction.
2. **Parent approval** — when Hostel Settings requires it and a student applied, the
   student's guardians who may approve leave are asked in the portal. A parent applying
   skips this step.
3. **Warden approval** — you see it in Approvals (status *Pending Warden Approval*). Approve
   or reject with remarks. The student and parents are notified.
4. If the student asked for a mess reduction and the leave is long enough (full mess days
   counted with a 12:00 noon cut-off), an approved leave creates the **Mess Reduction
   Application** automatically.
5. At the gate, security staff record **Checked Out** (from 2 hours before the leave starts)
   and **Returned**, at the actual time ([security staff manual](security-staff.md)).
6. If the student is not back by the return time plus the grace hours, the leave becomes
   **Overdue** (checked hourly) and you and the parents are notified.

On the leave form (*Student Services > Leave*) you can also **Approve** / **Reject**, record
**Checked Out** / **Returned** if the gate missed it, or **Cancel Leave** (*More*). A wardens'
leave for a student who did not apply can be entered on the desk with *Applied By* = Warden.

Reports: **Students On Leave** (who is away now), **Leave Register** (any period, export to
Excel).

## 3. Grievances

*Student Services > Grievances*. Students and parents raise grievances in the portal with a
category, priority, description and photos; you can also enter one on the desk.

- **SLA**: a grievance must be acted on within *Grievance Escalation Hours* (48), or 4 hours
  when Urgent. If not, it is escalated (hourly check), one level per period up to level 3,
  and the escalation goes to the Warden (and the deputy wardens of the hostel at level 1).
- **Ragging or Harassment** is always confidential, urgent and referred to the Warden at
  once. Confidential grievances are visible only to the Warden, administrators and the person
  assigned; parents see them only if they raised them.
- **Mess and Food** grievances are also visible to the mess manager.

Working a grievance:

1. **Assign** it to the staff member who will fix it (with a note). The assignee is notified.
2. **Add Update** to record progress: tick *Internal* for staff-only notes (never shown to
   the student), or leave it unticked so the student sees it on their timeline. You can
   change the status (In Progress) here.
3. **Resolve** with what was done. The student is notified and can rate the resolution, or
   **reopen** it within 7 days if the problem is back.
4. **Close** when the matter is finished.

Report: **Grievance Summary** (by category, status, SLA, ratings). **Feedback Summary** shows
the monthly feedback ratings and comments (anonymous feedback has no name).

## 4. Bonafide certificates

*Student Services > Bonafide Request*. The student requests a certificate in the portal with
a purpose (Bank Loan, Scholarship, Passport, Visa, Internship, Address Proof, Other).

1. **Approve** (or **Reject** with a reason). Approval gives a certificate number
   (`BC/YYYY/#####`), issue and validity dates and a verification code; the student can
   download the PDF from the portal at once.
2. **Download Certificate** prints it; the QR code on it opens the public verification page.
   **Copy Verification Link** gives the link for a bank or embassy that asks.
3. When a paper copy is handed over, the office marks it **Mark Handed Over** (status Issued).

Report: **Bonafide Register**.

## 5. Mess reductions and indents

- **Mess reductions** (if you approve them): Approvals > Mess Reduction, or *Mess >
  Reductions*. Check dates and reason; approve or reject with remarks. The rules (minimum
  days, notice, monthly cap) are checked when the student applies; you can approve a late
  application.
- **Indents** (Warden): Approvals > Mess Indents shows the items, estimated amount and the
  week's menu they are for. Indents above the threshold in Mess Settings must be approved by
  the Warden or an administrator.

## 6. Rooms and residents

- **Room Board** (*People and Rooms > Room Board*): every bed by hostel, block and floor, with
  filters by status and room type and a search by room, student or register number. Click a
  bed to allot it, transfer or vacate its student, reserve it or send it for maintenance.
- **Students** (*People and Rooms > Students*): the resident's record, guardians, current room
  and allotment history (*View > Allotment History*). Wardens and deputy wardens can read and
  update **Medical Notes**, which office staff cannot see.
- Registration, validation and fees are the office's work ([office staff manual](office-staff.md)).

## 7. Gate oversight

The **Gate Pass** page (*Student Services > Gate Pass*) shows students leaving today and
everyone away (overdue first) for your hostels. Use it to check who is out, and to record a
movement if the gate could not.

## 8. Notifications

You get desk notifications (bell icon) and, as configured, e-mail for: leave waiting for your
approval, new grievances in your hostels, assignments and escalations, bonafide requests, and
overdue returns.

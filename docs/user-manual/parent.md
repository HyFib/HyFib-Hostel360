# Parent guide

For parents and guardians of hostel residents. Follow your ward's fees, mess bills and
leave, approve their home-visit leave, and raise concerns — from your phone, in English or
தமிழ். Tamil quick guide: [மாணவர் / பெற்றோர் விரைவு வழிகாட்டி](student-parent-quick-guide-ta.md).

## 1. Open the portal and sign in

Go to **`https://<portal address>/hostel`** (for ACGCET, e.g. `https://hostel.accet.ac.in/hostel`),
or use the **Parent login** button on the hostel website.

1. Enter **your** mobile number — the one you gave the hostel office as your ward's parent
   or guardian — choose **SMS** or **WhatsApp**, tap **Send code**.
2. Enter the 6-digit code and tap **Verify and sign in** (valid 5 minutes; **Resend code**
   after 60 seconds).
3. If your ward uses the same number, choose **I am a parent or guardian**.
4. First time: read the **privacy notice** and tap **I have read and accept**.

The parent portal opens once the hostel office has validated your ward's registration. If it
says you cannot use the portal right now, contact the hostel office (phone and e-mail are on
the screen).

Never share your sign-in code. Hostel and HyFib staff will never ask for it.

## 2. More than one child in the hostel

The student switcher at the top ("Viewing … Switch student") shows the child you are looking at. Tap it to switch: fees,
leave, menu and requests then show that child's information. Notifications say which child
they are about.

## 3. Home

**Fees due** with **Pay now**, the child's **room**, **today's menu**, **Waiting for your
approval** (leave requests you need to decide), requests in progress and the latest updates.

## 4. Approve leave

When your ward applies for a home visit, you get a message and the request appears under
**Approvals** (bottom bar).

1. Open **Leave approvals**: dates, destination, reason, contact number, and whether a mess
   reduction was asked for.
2. **Approve**, or **Reject** with a reason (required). Add remarks for the warden if useful.
3. After your approval the warden decides; you are notified. You are told again when your ward
   checks out at the gate, when they are back, and if they are overdue.

Only guardians the hostel marked as able to approve leave are asked.

## 5. Apply for leave on your ward's behalf

**Leave** > **Apply**: dates and times (India time), destination, reason, contact number,
mess reduction if wanted > **Submit leave request**. Your own approval is not needed; the
warden decides.

## 6. Fees and mess bills

- **Fees**: outstanding and overdue amounts, every invoice with its items, and online payment
  (UPI, card, net banking) exactly as described in the [student guide](student.md#4-fees-and-payment).
  The receipt is issued at once and can be downloaded from **Receipts**.
- **Mess bills**: each month's bill with days, daily rate and extras, and whether it is paid.
- **Mess menu**: the week's menu.
- **Mess reduction**: see or apply for reduction days for your ward.

## 7. Grievances

You can raise a grievance about your ward's stay (**Grievances** > **Raise a grievance**),
follow it, comment, and rate it when resolved. Grievances your ward marked confidential are
not shown to you unless you raised them.

## 8. Certificates and notifications

- **Certificates**: request a bonafide certificate for your ward and download it once approved.
- **Notifications**: everything about your ward — dues, payments, leave decisions, gate
  check-out and return, menus, bills and grievance updates.

## 9. Help

**Help and support** (under More): the hostel office for anything about the stay (rooms,
fees, rules, emergencies), and HyFib portal support (+91 96060 60596, support@hyfib.com, 24×7)
for sign-in or payment problems. Keep the payment reference handy when asking about a
payment.

To leave the portal on a shared phone, **Sign out** on the Profile screen.
